Important exception paths

The application prevents silent loss of work when normal plans change.

No answerRecord outcome and schedule another call; task stays visible.
Dates unknownKeep date type as Unknown/Flexible; exact dates are optional.
Duplicate customerReview the warning before creating a separate record.
Staff unavailableOwner reassigns open enquiries and tasks; old notices are removed.
Imported row unclearMark Needs review and do not create active reminders.
Driver collects moneyDriver informs Owner; Owner records and confirms it in a later phase.
