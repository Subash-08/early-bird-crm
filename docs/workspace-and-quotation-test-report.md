# Workspace, quotation and package template rollout

## Follow-up checks for quotation requests and previews

1. Open an enquiry in **Gathering details**, then go to **Activity → Request quotation**. Fill any listed enquiry gaps and save. Submit the request and confirm the stage becomes **Quote requested**. Return to Activity and check that **View quotation** now opens the linked quotation list instead of offering a duplicate.
2. Apply the Kerala template to a saved draft for **Munnar and Alleppey**. Leave one property's hotel dates and the quotation validity blank. Verify the approval checklist names the stay and links to **Stays and rooms** and **Inclusions and terms**. Enter the planned check-in and check-out for each stay, set **Quotation valid until**, save the draft and approve as Owner.
3. Check that draft and approved previews show ₹49,340 and ₹52,750 for the unmodified Kerala options. Change one option's quantity, discount or tax in another test and check that the draft and approved totals match. Meals should appear as descriptions, with package options priced as a whole.
4. On mobile and desktop, check the full preview for named hotels and houseboat, room and meal details, transfer routes, day plans, inclusions, exclusions, and cancellation terms. Try a customer-booked transport leg and a template with optional extras, and confirm that private supplier costs, booking references and internal notes do not appear.

## What changed

- The desktop sidebar groups pages by Travel desk, Daily work and Agency setup. The mobile bottom navigation still leads to the same role-specific pages. The sidebar stays in place while switching pages.
- Owner has agency setup and shared work pages. Sales has shared work pages for authorized records and can apply active templates. Operations has Today, My trips, Tasks, Attendance, Notifications and Reports, with its existing record and report restrictions.
- Bookings, Operations and Money & changes have tighter spacing, clearer headings and distinct blue, green and purple accents. Supporting text, borders and keyboard focus are easier to see across the workspace.
- A quotation opens on its own URL from the quotation list, the enquiry or a quote alert. Saving keeps that page open. A revision opens its own URL. Existing Owner approval and assigned lead sharing permissions apply.
- In an enquiry, your own name is marked **(you)** in the Lead list. The team picker shows other active staff with an eligible role, excluding the current lead and existing teammates. The Owner keeps agency-wide enquiry access.
- New approved quotes keep option prices, inclusions, exclusions, validity and cancellation terms. They omit advance and balance terms from the editor and customer PDFs. Set an advance in **Bookings → Payments** if needed. Historical approved records keep the original terms and stored quotation PDF.
- Recording acceptance changes the quote to **Accepted**, closes any open enquiry follow-up and alerts the relevant team. Create the booking from the accepted quote. The quote and enquiry show **Trip completed** when the linked trip reaches that status in Operations.
- **Package templates** lists active and inactive templates. The Owner can search, create from scratch, edit and make a template inactive. Existing quotes keep their saved package data. Quote and enquiry-team notifications can open the specific record. Other notifications open their corresponding workspace.
