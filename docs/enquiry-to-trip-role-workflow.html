EARLYBIRD TRAVEL CRM

One enquiry → completed trip

This visual follows the permissions and gates implemented in the current code. Assignment, quotation approval, verified money, Operations release, trip completion and financial closure are deliberately separate.

Owner

Assigns, prices, approves, verifies money, releases Operations and closes finance.

Sales

Calls the customer, gathers details, requests/shares the quote and records decisions/evidence.

Operations

Executes assigned travel, readiness, times and incidents without seeing commercial data.

OwnerCapture and assign

Select the existing customer, capture the enquiry and assign the Sales user.

SalesCall and qualify

Record travellers, dates, hotel, services, pickup/drop, budget, next action and follow-up.

SalesRequest quotation

The system checks that enough information exists. Missing details return to Sales.

OwnerPrepare and approve

Create options, itinerary, price and terms; approve a locked version and generate the PDF.

SalesShare and record decision

Record manual sharing. Accept one approved option, request changes, or reject with a reason.

SalesCreate booking

Convert the accepted quote once and complete payer, traveller and coordination information.

OwnerIssue invoice

Review billing/tax lines and issue the immutable customer invoice.

SalesOwnerSubmit and verify advance

Sales submits evidence. Only Owner verification changes paid totals.

OwnerRelease and assign Operations

After the advance gate, assign staff, vehicles, segments and service checklist.

OperationsExecute the trip

Complete readiness and assigned segments; record times, notes and incidents.

OperationsComplete trip

Confirmed services, completed segments and resolved urgent incidents are required.

OwnerClose settlement

Resolve customer balance, supplier money, expenses, refunds and custody, then close finance.
